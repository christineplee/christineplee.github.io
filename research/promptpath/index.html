PromptPath

Christine P. Lee, Evan Pan, David Porfirio, and Bilge Mutlu. Under review.

PromptPath keeps a persistent profile of the user and brings it into the prompts they write. It rewrites a prompt by searching over candidate repairs, simulating how the user would react to each one. Try it by building a short profile and asking for trip ideas.

The LLM's responses are scripted for this demo, and the UCB1 search and source analysis run live in your browser.

The task: a short travel prompt

Short prompts leave out what the model needs to know about you. PromptPath first learns about you through a chat and saves what it learns as a profile. When you write a prompt later, it uses that profile to repair the prompt before it goes to the LLM.

Answer three questions, then ask: "I want to go somewhere warm and interesting in October, not too crowded."

ProfilePreferences, needs, and goals from the chat, saved and reused for every later prompt.
Prompt repairCandidate rewrites are scored by simulating your feedback, and a UCB1 search picks the best one.
