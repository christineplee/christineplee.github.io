Communication Initialization Profile (CIP)

What is the least an LLM needs to know about someone to adapt how it communicates with them?

CIP is our answer for the start of an interaction, before the model has any history with a person: 16 items that go into the system prompt, so the model can adapt its tone, level of detail, structure, and initiative.
