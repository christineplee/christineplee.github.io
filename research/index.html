Specify, check, and revise

AI agents make many choices on people's behalf that people do not see. I make a person's intent explicit and shared with the agent, so people can:

  1. Specify what matters
  2. Check the agent's choices against it
  3. Revise it as things change

Each system has a demo you can try.
