MAP

Christine P. Lee*, Jihye Choi*, and Bilge Mutlu (* equal contribution). CHI 2025 Extended Abstracts. PaperPDFCode

MAP coordinates LLM-powered agents to personalize for several people at once. It gathers each person's rules, plans around the conflicts between them, explains its choices, and takes feedback. Try it on the assisted care scenario from the paper.

The agents' responses are scripted for this demo. The residents' documents and the rule for resolving conflicts are the ones the system used.

The task: assisted care

A care facility's robot looks after three residents, Blaine, Ryan, and Susie. Each gave it a document of preferences and schedules, 20 rules each. Their needs often overlap, and when they do, the facility's rule is to serve residents in alphabetical order of their first names.

Ask the robot how it will care for them.
